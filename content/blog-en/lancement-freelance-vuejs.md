Registering as a sole proprietor is the easy part. Finding your first clients and positioning yourself clearly is much harder.

## The trap of overly broad positioning

"Full-stack Vue.js developer available for any kind of project" speaks to no one. Precise positioning, even if it reduces the number of potential clients on paper, increases the response rate because a client looking for exactly that profile immediately knows it is a match.

## Cold outreach without making it feel cold

A prospecting message that looks like a template is obvious from the first line. What works better is mentioning a specific, verifiable detail about the target company, showing that the message was not sent to fifty people at the same time.

## Administration should never block development

Invoices, quotes, and tax declarations: anything that can be turned into a template should be from the first client, not the tenth. The time saved later more than makes up for the initial investment.

## What I would change

Start prospecting before the administrative process is complete, not after. The gap between "I am ready to work" and "I have my first clients" is almost always longer than expected.
