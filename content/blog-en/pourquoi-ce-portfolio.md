A portfolio that only shows screenshots of finished projects tells an incomplete story. It leaves out everything that happened between the beginning and the end: the decisions, mistakes, and changes of direction.

## The idea behind the blog

The goal is to document the technical decisions made on my projects in public, including those that turned out to be mistakes. It is more honest, and often more useful to someone looking for the same answer, than yet another generic article on the subject.

## What you will find here

Concrete lessons learned: architecture, tool choices, trade-offs between development speed and quality, and the lessons drawn once the project is in production.
